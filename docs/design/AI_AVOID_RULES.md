# AI Design Avoid Rules


Never use:


## Colors

❌Purple gradient
❌ Blue-purple combination
❌ Random accent colors
❌ More than 5 colors


## Cards

Avoid:

❌ Excessive rounded cards
❌ Floating cards everywhere
❌ Card inside card


## Shadows

Allowed:

Subtle shadow only:

box-shadow:
0 1px 3px rgba(...)


Avoid:

Large shadows
Glow effects


## Layout

Avoid:

❌ Center everything
❌ Huge empty spaces
❌ Marketing landing page style


Prefer:

Enterprise dashboard layout.
